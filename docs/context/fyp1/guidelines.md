## 1. General Project Constraints (Development-Based)

*   **Project Nature**: Involves the design and construction of a non-trivial prototype/application. The development should follow an established software/data engineering method.
*   **Core Deliverables to Address**:
    *   The purpose of the software/architecture.
    *   In what way the project is novel.
    *   Underlying theories/concepts underpinning the project.
    *   Applicable engineering methods and tools to be used.
    *   Methods envisaged for testing and evaluating the system.

## 2. Report Structure & Chapter Arrangement

The proposal should be between **20 to 50 pages** (excluding tables and figures). Each chapter must begin on a new page. Subsections are limited to 3 levels.

### 2.1 Preliminary Sections
1.  **Title Page**: Must strictly adhere to the template format. Change the word "REPORT" to "PROPOSAL".
2.  **Acknowledgements**: Maximum 1 page.
3.  **Copyright Statement**: 1 page.
4.  **Abstract**: Maximum 1 page (150 - 300 words). Includes field of study, problem definition, methodology, research process, and conclusion. Must include **Area of Study** (Min 1, Max 2) and **Keywords** (Min 5, Max 10).
5.  **Table of Contents, List of Tables, List of Figures, List of Symbols, List of Abbreviations**.

### 2.2 Core Chapters

#### Chapter 1: Introduction (5 to 10 pages)
*   **Problem Statement and Motivation (1-2 paragraphs)**: State the existing problem and why the project is needed. Justify the existence of the project.
*   **Project Scope (1-2 paragraphs)**: Describe what will be delivered at the end of the project. Define boundaries.
*   **Project Objectives (1-4 paragraphs)**: The main objective and measurable sub-objectives.
*   **Impact, Significance and Contribution (1-2 paragraphs)**: How the project benefits society, the target audience, or the industry.
*   **Background Information (>3 paragraphs)**: Historical development, key technical terms, and foundational knowledge needed to understand the project.

#### Chapter 2: Literature Review (5 to 20 pages)
*   **Literature Review**: Highlight current practices or prior arts towards the problem.
*   **Critical Remarks of Previous Works**: Compare existing solutions/architectures with your proposed solutions. Highlight strengths and limitations.

#### Chapter 3: Proposed Method/Approach (3 to 20 pages)
*   **Design Specifications**: Methodologies and General Work Procedures. System Performance Definition (targeted improvements like latency, accuracy) and Verification Plan.
*   **System Design / Overview**: Top-down system design diagrams (architecture, flowcharts). Detail the functionality of each component/block.
*   **Implementation Issues and Challenges (1-2 paragraphs)**: Anticipated difficult issues.
*   **Novel aspects of this project**.
*   **Timeline (1 paragraph)**: Estimated timeline for deliverables (Gantt chart format).

#### Chapter 4: Preliminary Work (1 to 3 pages)
*   Report the preliminary setup, initial scripts, or early results obtained according to the proposed method.
*   Comment on the feasibility of the proposed method based on these preliminary results.

#### Chapter 5: Conclusion (1 to 3 pages)
*   Summarize the project (problem, motivation, proposed solutions).
*   No new ideas or information should be introduced here.

### 2.3 Post-Body Sections
*   **References**: 10 to 50 references (IEEE Style).
*   **Appendices**: Supplementary materials (specifications, data sheets, charts, code snippets).

## 3. Formatting & Typesetting Specifications

*   **Writing Style**: Third person and past tense (No "I" or "you").
*   **Language**: British English.
*   **Font**: Times New Roman, 12 points, 1.5 line spacing. (Exceptions: Header, Footer, Footnote, Figure/Table text should be 10-11 points).
*   **Margins**: Left 1 inch (except front cover 1.2 inches); Right, Top, Bottom 1 inch; Header 0.5 inch, Footer 0.4 inch.
*   **Header**: Align left: chapter number and title.
*   **Footer**: Align right: page number. Align left: Degree string & Faculty string.
*   **Page Numbering**: 
    *   Preliminaries: small Roman numerals (i, ii, iii...). 
    *   Chapters & References: Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3...). 
    *   Appendices: A-1, A-2...
*   **Tables and Figures**: Numbered sequentially per chapter (e.g., Table 2.2). Caption immediately **below** the figure/table.
*   **Equations**: Typed in *Italic*. Centered, numbered right-aligned. Use `(y/x) = ax + b`.
